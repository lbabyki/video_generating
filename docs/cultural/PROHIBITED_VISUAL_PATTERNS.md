# Prohibited Visual Patterns

- Unverified flags, maps, seals, symbols, or boundary claims.
- Text generated by diffusion, including labels, captions, and signs.
- Scanned textbook/SGK artwork as training data without explicit rights.
- Real pupil photos or likenesses without appropriate documented permission.
- Generic Asian substitutions: foreign palace, shrine, gate, costume, or landscape.
- Decorative default use of áo dài or nón lá without story context.
