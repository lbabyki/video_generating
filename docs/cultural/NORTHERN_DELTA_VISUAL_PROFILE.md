# Northern Delta Visual Profile v1.0

For Đồng bằng Bắc Bộ use low alluvial rice fields, dikes, canals or ponds, village paths, tiled-roof homes where evidence supports them, humid seasonal light, and age-appropriate modern rural life. Avoid mountains, coastal dunes, tropical palms as default, highland longhouses, Japanese gates, Chinese palace motifs, or generic pan-Asian villages. Verify landmarks before use. Áo dài and nón lá need story context and are not default costume or shorthand.
